Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Who we are

Learn In Minutes is a YouTube channel that publishes short technology news videos. This policy covers the "LIM YouTube Studio" application, the internal tool the channel uses to publish its own videos.

What the application does

The application is used only by the Learn In Minutes team, on the Learn In Minutes YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube Data API to upload our own videos, set their titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and post and manage comments on our own channel.

What data we collect

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data about viewers or any other Google users. The application accesses only the Learn In Minutes channel that authorized it. OAuth credentials for that channel are stored securely on our own infrastructure and are used solely to publish and manage the channel's content.

Google API Services

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We also follow the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions.
